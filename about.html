About Next in Dhaka

Next in Dhaka is a digital guide to what is happening in the city: concerts, expos, exhibitions, launches, property fairs, festivals and business meetups.

What we do

We help people find events worth their time, and we help organizers reach an audience that is actually looking. We also publish short stories and guides about the city’s events scene.

Status

This website is an early prototype. All events and articles shown are fictional samples used to test the design.
